NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's statement on why open models matter for AI development and American technological leadership.

## Key Points

- AI will transform every industry, power every company, and be built by every country
- Open models strengthen safety and cybersecurity by enabling transparency and community review
- Open models accelerate innovation and diffusion across the industry and globally
- Open models enable sovereignty — allowing countries to build AI on their own terms
- Both frontier closed models AND frontier open models are needed for a healthy AI ecosystem

## Why This Matters

This letter represents a major technology company's endorsement of open-source AI development, arguing that it's not only beneficial for innovation but essential for safety, security, and geopolitical independence.
