During a State Department presentation at a global conference in Brazil, the U.S. government displayed a map of Africa with every single country mislabeled. The State Department took full responsibility for the embarrassing error, which was widely shared and criticized on social media.

The incident raised questions about the quality of preparation for international diplomatic events and the State Department's attention to detail in official presentations.
