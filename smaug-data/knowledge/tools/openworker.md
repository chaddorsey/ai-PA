OpenWorker is an open-source AI coworker that runs on your desktop (macOS and Windows) and delivers **finished work**, not just chat. It breaks down complex tasks and works across your files and everyday applications to produce polished deliverables like documents, reports, spreadsheets, and calendar updates.

## Key Features

- **Delivers finished work** - Documents, spreadsheets, reports, and web pages land as files you can open and share
- **Model-agnostic** - Bring your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or open-weight providers; fully local support via Ollama
- **Privacy-first** - Runs entirely on your machine; data only leaves through your chosen LLM provider and integrations
- **Desktop-based** - Available for macOS (Apple Silicon); Windows support coming soon
- **25+ tool integrations** - GitHub, Slack, Jira, Notion, Linear, HubSpot, Outlook, Google Calendar, terminal, and local files
- **Approval-gated actions** - Requests your confirmation before sending messages, changing calendars, or running commands
- **Scheduled automations** - Set up recurring work like morning briefs, weekly reports, or channel monitoring

## Use Cases

- Prepare customer briefs and reports
- Untangle and organize calendars
- Draft documents and proposals
- Triage Slack and email inboxes
- Automate recurring business tasks
- Cross-tool data retrieval and synthesis

## Model Support

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Inkling, GLM, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, MiniMax, Mistral, Grok, plus open-weight models via Together/Fireworks and local models via Ollama.
