# Task 29-7: Create insight templates

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Create template-based insights for common game situations, enabling fast-path insight generation without LLM calls.

|-----------|------------|-------------|-----------|---------|------|

## Requirements

1. **Situation Templates**
   - Third down explanations (short, medium, long)
   - Fourth down decision context
   - Red zone entry
   - Two-minute warning
   - Timeout usage

2. **Play Type Templates**
   - Big play celebration (20+ yards)
   - Turnover explanation
   - Scoring play context
   - Penalty explanations

3. **Game State Templates**
   - Momentum shift alerts
   - Score situation context
   - Clock management observations

4. **Template Format**
   - YAML-based for easy editing
   - Variable substitution support
   - Priority and timing metadata

## Implementation Plan

### Template Structure

```yaml
# insights.yaml

situation_templates:
  third_down:
    short:  # 1-3 yards
      priority: 7
      timing: pre_snap
      ttl: 15
      headline: "3rd and {distance}—short yardage"
      body: |
        Only need {distance} yard{s} for a first down. 
        Run is likely here, but watch for play-action—
        the defense will crowd the line expecting run.
      conditions:
        down: 3
        distance_max: 3
    
    medium:  # 4-6 yards
      priority: 6
      timing: pre_snap
      ttl: 15
      headline: "3rd and {distance}—manageable"
      body: |
        Need {distance} yards. This is typically a passing down.
        Watch for a quick route designed to hit right at the marker.
      conditions:
        down: 3
        distance_min: 4
        distance_max: 6
    
    long:  # 7+ yards
      priority: 6
      timing: pre_snap
      ttl: 15
      headline: "3rd and {distance}—tough conversion"
      body: |
        {distance} yards is a lot to ask. Teams convert only about 
        25% of these. Watch for a defensive blitz—they smell a punt.
      conditions:
        down: 3
        distance_min: 7

  fourth_down:
    go_for_it:
      priority: 8
      timing: pre_snap
      ttl: 20
      headline: "Going for it on 4th!"
      body: |
        Bold call. Analytics says the expected points from trying 
        exceed punting here. Watch for their best short-yardage play.
      conditions:
        down: 4
        no_punt_formation: true
    
    punt:
      priority: 5
      timing: pre_snap
      ttl: 15
      headline: "Punt coming"
      body: |
        Field position game. They'll try to pin {opponent} deep.
      conditions:
        down: 4
        punt_formation: true

  red_zone:
    entry:
      priority: 7
      timing: post_play
      ttl: 30
      headline: "Red zone—scoring territory"
      body: |
        Inside the 20. Teams score touchdowns about 60% of the time 
        they reach the red zone. The compressed field helps the defense.
      conditions:
        entering_red_zone: true

  two_minute_warning:
    first_half:
      priority: 6
      timing: stoppage
      ttl: 60
      headline: "Two-minute warning"
      body: |
        Automatic timeout at 2:00. {possession_team} has the ball 
        with a chance to score before halftime. Watch for tempo changes.
      conditions:
        quarter: 2
        two_minute_warning: true
    
    second_half:
      priority: 8
      timing: stoppage
      ttl: 60
      headline: "Two-minute warning—crunch time"
      body: |
        This is where games are won and lost. {possession_team} 
        {situation_context}. Every play matters now.
      conditions:
        quarter: 4
        two_minute_warning: true

play_templates:
  big_play:
    priority: 8
    timing: post_play
    ttl: 20
    headline: "Big gain: {yards} yards!"
    body: |
      {play_description}. {player_name} {play_context}.
    conditions:
      yards_gained_min: 20

  turnover:
    interception:
      priority: 9
      timing: immediate
      ttl: 30
      headline: "INTERCEPTION!"
      body: |
        {defender} picks it off! {context}. 
        That's a huge momentum swing.
      conditions:
        play_type: interception
    
    fumble:
      priority: 9
      timing: immediate
      ttl: 30
      headline: "FUMBLE!"
      body: |
        {player} loses the ball and {recovering_team} recovers! 
        {context}
      conditions:
        play_type: fumble_lost

  touchdown:
    priority: 9
    timing: immediate
    ttl: 45
    headline: "TOUCHDOWN {team}!"
    body: |
      {play_description}. {scoring_context}
      Score: {home_team} {home_score}, {away_team} {away_score}
    conditions:
      scoring_play: true
      play_result: touchdown

  penalty:
    common:
      priority: 5
      timing: post_play
      ttl: 25
      headline: "Flag: {penalty_name}"
      body: |
        {penalty_description}. {yards} yard penalty on {team}.
        {rule_explanation}
    offensive_penalties:
      false_start: "Offensive lineman moved before the snap. Free 5 yards for the defense."
      holding: "Lineman grabbed a defender illegally. Often called on big gains—erases the play."
      illegal_formation: "Not enough players on the line of scrimmage at the snap."
    defensive_penalties:
      offsides: "Defender crossed the line before the snap. Free 5 yards and a free play if offense snapped it."
      pass_interference: "Defender interfered with receiver's ability to catch the ball. Spot foul in the NFL."
      roughing_passer: "Hit the quarterback illegally. Automatic first down."

game_state_templates:
  momentum_shift:
    priority: 7
    timing: post_play
    ttl: 45
    headline: "Momentum swinging to {team}"
    body: |
      {context}. Win probability shifted {shift_amount}% in favor of {team}.
    conditions:
      win_prob_shift_min: 10

  blowout_developing:
    priority: 4
    timing: stoppage
    ttl: 120
    headline: "Game getting away from {trailing_team}"
    body: |
      Down {margin} in the {quarter} quarter. 
      They'll need to score quickly to have a chance.
    conditions:
      score_margin_min: 21
      quarter_min: 3

  comeback_brewing:
    priority: 7
    timing: post_play
    ttl: 60
    headline: "Comeback watch"
    body: |
      {trailing_team} has cut the lead to {margin}. 
      {time_context}
    conditions:
      score_margin_decreased: true
      margin_max: 14
      quarter: 4
```
